# 🧠 Key Insights
## 💰 Revenue & Refund Overview
1. Strong overall revenue performance with meaningful refund impact.
- Total revenue is $10.64M while total refund is $894K, meaning refunds are a noticeable hit to performance and should be monitored as a cost driver.
2. Revenue shows clear seasonality with major spikes during peak months.
- The monthly revenue trend has multiple sharp peaks, suggesting demand is not evenly distributed and is influenced by seasonal shopping behaviors, which is during the final quarter of the year.  
3. Refund follows a similar pattern but at a lower magnitude.
- The monthly refund trend rise in the same general time period as revenue, indicating returns directly proportion with sales volume.
## 📈 Product Performance
4. High-revenue products are not always the highest-volume products.
- The top products by revenue match the top products by quantity, but not perfectly, suggesting a mix of high price/low volume items and low price/high volume items driving results.
5. Refund is condensed in a small set of products.
- The top refunded items include non-product charges like AMAZON FEE and a few repeated product names, suggesting quality issues or order accuracy problems may be concentrated rather than spread across all products.
6. Postage ranks among the top revenue products, showing revenue is not just product-based.
- Products like POSTAGE and DOTCOM POSTAGE appear in the top products by revenue list, meaning non-product charges contribute heavily and should be separated from true product performance when making inventory decisions. 
## 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Customer Insights
7. The customer base is heavily dominated by repeat customers.
- Repeat customers almost double one-time customers, which is a strong sign of retention and recurring purchasing behavior.
8. Revenue is driven mostly by known customers rather than guest checkouts.
- Known customers generate the majority of revenue, meaning customer tracking is strong and enables deeper segmentation strategies (VIP targeting, loyalty, email campaigns).
9. Revenue is concentrated among a small group of top customers.
- The “Top 10 customers by revenue” chart shows a steep drop after the highest customers, suggesting customer concentration risk - a small group contributes a large share of revenue.
